Mission & Vision

Dermatology That Puts People Before Protocols

Our mission is to deliver evidence-based dermatological care with the warmth of a boutique wellness studio — combining rigorous medical standards with genuine, unhurried attention to every patient's story.

We envision a future where advanced skin care is approachable, transparent and free of judgment — for every skin tone, age and condition.

Our Mission

Accessible, evidence-based dermatology delivered with genuine care.

Our Vision

A world where confident, healthy skin is within everyone's reach.

Our Journey

Two Decades Of Growth & Trust

2007

Solene Opens Its First Clinic

Founded by Dr. Elena Marsh with a single treatment room and a promise: never rush a diagnosis.

2013

Launch Of Laser & Cosmetic Division

Introduced advanced laser therapy and cosmetic dermatology under one integrated roof.

2019

Digital Patient Dashboard Introduced

Patients gained secure online access to appointments, prescriptions and treatment history.

2026

24,000+ Patients & Counting

Today, Solene operates with 12 specialist doctors and a 97% patient satisfaction rate.
