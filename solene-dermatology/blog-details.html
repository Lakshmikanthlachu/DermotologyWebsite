Building A Dermatologist-Approved Morning Routine

Dr. Sofia Reyes

Aug 12, 2026 · 5 min read

A good morning skincare routine doesn't need twelve steps — it needs the right four. As dermatologists, we see patients layering expensive serums onto compromised skin barriers every day. Here's the routine we actually recommend to our own patients.

1. A Gentle, pH-Balanced Cleanser

Your morning cleanse isn't removing makeup or sunscreen — it's simply clearing overnight oil and sweat. A harsh cleanser here does more harm than good, stripping the skin barrier and triggering excess oil production later in the day.

"The goal of a morning routine isn't more products — it's protecting what your skin repaired overnight." — Dr. Sofia Reyes

2. An Antioxidant Serum

A vitamin C or niacinamide serum in the morning helps neutralize environmental free-radical damage from UV and pollution exposure throughout the day, while also supporting an even skin tone over time.

3. A Lightweight Moisturizer

Moisturizer seals in your serum and reinforces your skin barrier. Look for ceramides and hyaluronic acid rather than heavy occlusives, which can feel greasy under sunscreen and makeup.

4. Broad-Spectrum SPF 30+

This is the single most important anti-aging step in dermatology — full stop. Apply a nickel-sized amount every morning, rain or shine, and reapply if you're outdoors for extended periods.
