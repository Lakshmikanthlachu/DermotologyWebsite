About Solene

A Clinic Built Around You

Founded in 2007, Solene Dermatology & Skin Clinic was created to close the gap between clinical-grade dermatology and a genuinely warm patient experience. Every treatment room is designed to feel like a retreat, not a hospital.

  • Board-certified specialists across every subspecialty of dermatology.
  • Advanced diagnostic technology including AI-assisted skin mapping.
  • Private, judgment-free care with flexible scheduling and telehealth.
